STUDENT LIFE

Student Activities

Our student activities at O.B. Montessori Center help develop our students’ personalities so they grow up confident, responsible, and well-rounded individuals. These activities allow students to discover their talents, explore their interests, build friendships, and learn how to work together with others. They also teach important values such as teamwork, discipline, leadership, respect, and cooperation. Through different experiences both inside and outside the classroom, students are encouraged to express themselves, overcome challenges, and become more independent. These activities help prepare them not only for school but also for their future lives.

CAMPING

During the Grade 6 and Grade 10 camping activities, students get the opportunity to think freely, experience new things, and participate in different activities as a team. These camps encourage students to step out of their comfort zones, become more independent, and develop their problem-solving skills. They also help students strengthen their friendships, practice teamwork, and learn the importance of cooperation, responsibility, and trust. Through these experiences, students create memorable moments while learning valuable life skills beyond the classroom.

Intramurals

Sports are an integral part of our programs at O.B. Montessori Center. We offer a variety of sports that promote physical fitness, teamwork, discipline, and a healthy lifestyle. Through these activities, students learn the importance of cooperation, sportsmanship, perseverance, and respecting others. Sports also allow students to discover their athletic abilities, build confidence, challenge themselves, and develop friendships with their fellow students. By participating in different sports, they learn how to work together, overcome challenges, and maintain a balance between their academic and physical development.

Marching Band

Our music events allow students to develop their musical abilities, from learning different instruments to participating in the school band. These activities encourage creativity, self-expression, discipline, and confidence while giving students the opportunity to explore their passion for music. Through rehearsals and performances, they also learn teamwork, cooperation, dedication, and how to work together to create something meaningful. Such experiences help students improve their skills while making lasting memories and discovering their talents.
